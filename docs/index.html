Regime Classification

14-period ATR, expressed as a percentage of price, routes to one of three regimes.

Range-Bound
ATR ≤ 1% of price. Grid / mean-reversion: buy at support, sell at resistance.
Trending
1% < ATR < 3% of price. Fast(10)/Slow(30) moving-average crossover.
Standby
ATR ≥ 3% of price. Volatility spike — no new entries.

AI Strategy Validation

How a signal earns the right to become a live paper trade — see the "AI reasoning" row above for the current cycle's verdict.

1. Propose
An AI model reads recent price action and live headlines from CNBC, Yahoo Finance, Bloomberg, WSJ, the NYT, and the Financial Times, then proposes one strategy (MA crossover, support/resistance, or RSI threshold) with parameters, plus a buy/sell/hold call.
2. Backtest
That exact strategy is replayed bar-by-bar over recent candle history, with the same trading fees and slippage as live trades, to get a trade count, win rate, and net return.
3. Execute (or hold)
The call only reaches a live paper trade if the backtest shows a positive net return across enough trades to trust it. Otherwise the bot holds — regardless of how confident the AI sounded.
